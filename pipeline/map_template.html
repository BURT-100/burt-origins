      How the numbers were made

      • Source: UltraSignup results for each BURT 100 distance. 2023 was a 55K-only year on UltraSignup, and no earlier editions are listed.
      • An entrant is anyone in the results: finishers, DNFs and, unless you turn them off, registered non-starters.
      • Hometown is what the runner entered at registration. Distances run from the town's center to Battle Point Park, so island runners all show about 4 miles.
      • Counts are by town only. No runner names are shown.
